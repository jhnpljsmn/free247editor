# Survive The Choice: series bible

Channel: **Survive The Choice** (@SurviveTheChoiceTV). Stickman zombie survival where **you** are
the hero and the comments decide what happens next. Built from the viral Shorts research of
2026-11-11 (`node src/cli.mjs research`): copy formats, never specific videos, stories or characters.

## Two formats

| | A: Day N | B: Would You Survive? |
|---|---|---|
| Project | `stc-dayNN` | `stc-wysNN` |
| Length | 2:00–2:50 (hard limit 3:00) | 20–30 s |
| Images | ~50 (cut every 2–3 s) | 8–10 |
| Ending | a 2-way vote: "Comment ROOF or STAIRS" | 3 numbered choices: "Comment 1, 2 or 3" |
| Next | the most-liked comment decides Day N+1 | the answer opens the next B Short |

Cadence: A twice a week (e.g. Mon, Thu), B on the days between. Every B ends by pointing to the
next Day N episode.

## Voice rules (both formats)

- **Second person, present tense.** "You wake up…", never "Paul wakes up". One narrator.
- First sentence = danger + a specific detail. No greeting, no channel name, no recap.
- A countdown or a stake in the first 10 seconds ("you have 20 minutes before…").
- Short sentences. One idea per caption.
- Every episode ends on an open question for the comments. Never resolve the vote in the same episode.

## Hook formulas (write our own words each time)

1. **Wrong-sounding rule:** "If [disaster] hits while you're in [place], don't [obvious move].
   [Why it's the right place], unless you make one mistake."
2. **Waking into chaos:** "You wake up [specific place] and [sensory chaos]. Then [exact alert or message]."
3. **Secret plus countdown:** "You're hiding [secret], and you have [time] before [consequence]."
4. **Choice:** "You have [time]. Which of these [N] [options] saves you?"

Rotate them; don't open two episodes in a row with the same formula.

## Season 1: the tower

You live on floor 27 of a 40-floor high-rise when the outbreak starts. The spine is fixed; votes
change how you get through each day and who survives.

| Day | Goal | Example vote |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first hour: get safe | Barricade your unit, or run for the roof? |
| 2 | Water runs out | Raid the empty units, or the tank on the roof? |
| 3 | Someone knocks | Open the door, or stay silent? |
| 4 | The first loss | Go back for them, or keep moving? |
| 5 | A radio signal | Answer it, or stay hidden? |
| 6 | The supply run, down to the ground-floor shop | Elevator, or the stairs? |
| 7 | The bite | Tell the group, or hide it? |
| 8 | The betrayal | Confront them, or leave quietly? |
| 9 | A way out appears | The helicopter on the roof, or the boat by the river? |
| 10 | The escape | Season vote: what the next location is |

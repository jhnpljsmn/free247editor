# Zombie Outbreak: Test Episode (Paul & Jen): voiceover

Eleven v3, same narrator voice for every scene. Paste the last line of each block; save as the file name shown
in `projects/test-paul-jen/assets/voiceovers/`.

### scene-01.mp3: Day 1
Delivery: calm, low, slow. A short pause after "Day one".
[calm] Day one... The city went quiet after midnight.

### scene-02.mp3: The Walk Home
Delivery: relaxed at first, a hint of unease on "flicker".
Paul and Jen were walking home when the streetlights began to [hesitant] flicker.

### scene-03.mp3: Something Moves
Delivery: quieter, tense, almost a whisper by the end.
Jen raised her flashlight. [whispers] At the end of the alley, something was moving toward them.

### scene-04.mp3: Run
Delivery: fast and urgent, hard stop on "ran".
[urgent] Paul saw them first. He pointed, and they ran.

### scene-05.mp3: Safe for Now
Delivery: out of breath at first, then softer, uneasy.
[breathless] They locked the door behind them. [softly] Jen kept looking back, but nothing followed.

### scene-06.mp3: The Knock
Delivery: slow build, long pause before "knocked".
[tense] Then... something knocked on the door.
